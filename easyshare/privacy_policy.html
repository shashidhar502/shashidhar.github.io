💬 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2024

Simple Summary: EasyShare does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data with external servers. All your snippets, contacts, and history live only on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how EasyShare ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application.

Developer: EasyLife
App Name: EasyShare
Package Name: com.easylife.easyshare

2. Information We Collect

2.1 What We DON'T Collect

EasyShare does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information, including:

  • ❌ Personal identification information
  • ❌ Phone numbers you enter or save
  • ❌ Snippets or messages you create
  • ❌ Contacts saved in the app
  • ❌ Your send history
  • ❌ Device information or identifiers
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Usage statistics or analytics
  • ❌ Crash reports

2.2 What Stays on Your Device

All data you enter in EasyShare is stored exclusively on your device using local Android storage (Room Database and SharedPreferences):

  • ✓ Snippets (Location, Address, Bank Account, Plain Text)
  • ✓ In-app contacts
  • ✓ Send history
  • ✓ App preferences (default country code, default snippets)

None of this data ever leaves your device.

3. How EasyShare Works

  1. You enter or paste a WhatsApp number on the Home screen
  2. You select which snippets to send from your personal library
  3. EasyShare opens WhatsApp with the message pre-filled using the wa.me deep link
  4. You review and send the message in WhatsApp

No message content is sent to EasyShare's servers — the app simply passes the text to WhatsApp via a standard Android intent.

4. Internet Permission

EasyShare declares the INTERNET permission in its manifest. This permission is used exclusively to open WhatsApp web links (https://wa.me/...) via the Android browser or WhatsApp app. It is not used to transmit any data to external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

EasyShare does NOT use any third-party SDKs or services, including:

  • ❌ No analytics (Firebase, Google Analytics, etc.)
  • ❌ No advertising networks
  • ❌ No crash reporting services
  • ❌ No cloud sync or backup services
  • ❌ No authentication services

When you send a message, WhatsApp is opened externally. Your interactions within WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp's Privacy Policy.

6. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET — Only to open WhatsApp wa.me links. No data is transmitted.

EasyShare does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, storage, location, or any other sensitive permission.

7. Data Storage & Security

7.1 Local Storage Only

All data is stored in your device's private app storage using Android Room Database. It is not accessible to other apps and is deleted when you uninstall EasyShare.

7.2 Security

  • ✓ No data is transmitted — it cannot be intercepted
  • ✓ No external servers — your data cannot be accessed remotely
  • ✓ Your data is protected by your device's security (PIN, biometrics)

8. Children's Privacy

EasyShare is safe for all ages. Since we do not collect any information from anyone, there are no COPPA concerns. The app is rated Everyone on Google Play.

9. Your Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and similar privacy laws, you have rights over your personal data. Since EasyShare stores all data locally on your device, you have full control:

  • ✓ Access: All your data is visible directly in the app
  • ✓ Deletion: Delete snippets, contacts, or history from within the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.
  • ✓ Portability: Your data never left your device
  • ✓ Rectification: Edit any snippet or contact directly in the app

10. Data Retention

EasyShare retains no data on external servers. Data stored locally on your device persists until you delete it within the app or uninstall the app.

11. International Data Transfers

Since EasyShare does not collect or transmit any data, there are no international data transfers.
